Source and attribution
----------------------
The monument records, descriptions and images from which the Nepal release of HeritageGraph is
built are the work of the Digital Archive of Nepalese Arts and Monuments (DANAM),
Heidelberg University (https://danam.cats.uni-heidelberg.de). Every monument in the
graph links to the DANAM page it was derived from. Credit DANAM as the source of
the content and CAIR-Nepal for the knowledge graph.
